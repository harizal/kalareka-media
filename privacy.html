Kala Reka – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Kala Reka is an educational video channel. This app ("Kala Reka Tools") is used only by the channel owner to publish and schedule the channel's own videos on the Kala Reka Facebook Page, Instagram account and Threads account, and to read and reply to comments on those posts.

What data the app uses

  • Access tokens for the channel owner's own Facebook Page, Instagram account and Threads account, stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Public comments left on Kala Reka's own posts (commenter name or username and comment text), read so the owner can answer them.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data from people who log in, because nobody other than the channel owner uses the app.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Comment text is kept only in the owner's private working files for as long as needed to reply, and is deleted on request. See the data deletion instructions.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: send a message to the Kala Reka Facebook Page (facebook.com, Page "Kala Reka") or to the contact email listed on the Kala Reka YouTube channel.
