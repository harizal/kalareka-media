Kala Reka – Data Deletion Instructions

The Kala Reka Tools app is used only by the Kala Reka channel owner. It does not store data about other Facebook, Instagram or Threads users, except the text of public comments left on Kala Reka's own posts, kept so the owner can reply.

To ask for your comment data to be deleted:

  1. Send a message to the Kala Reka Facebook Page, or to the contact email listed on the Kala Reka YouTube channel, with the subject "Data deletion".
  2. Include your username and the post you commented on.
  3. We delete the stored copy within 30 days and confirm by reply.

You can also remove the app's access at any time in Facebook: Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites.
